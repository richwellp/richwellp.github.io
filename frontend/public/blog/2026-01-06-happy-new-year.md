The purpose of this blog post is the write a reflection about my decision to pursue Computer Science. 

## Introduced to Computer Science

What do you want to be when you grow up? A dentist. Soldier. Pilot. Astronaut. These what I always said when I was growing up just because they were cool.

My interest began when I was in 7th grade (2012), when I saw my older brother and relatives doing fancy computer stuff that I could not comprehend but thought it was cool. My cousin made me type my Facebook password, and he was able to unblur my Facebook password only for the witnesses to read that my password was just my name.

The Philippines has slow internet speeds. My brother would always download us shows through the internet, and he built a website project for watching and listing anime shows. The internet at the time was still emerging, and I thought it was really cool (x2).

With these experience, Computer Science became my (realistic) placeholder for what I wanted to be when I grow up. 

## Entering Highschool

My parents migrated to the United States in 2012, and by 2013, my siblings and I moved as well to Illinois. Around this age is when kids are asked to make decisions that will shape their future. I was truly aimless and was never a good student. Migrating to the US was a chance for me to be a different person. I wanted to be good in school and smarter. I went through cringe stages or 8th grade syndrome, and I was like that until I had fully matured. From a kid who is charismatic and can be friends with anyone to someone who developed social anxiety. 

We were asked to find jobs and research career prospects in school. I was always asked to consider becoming a Nurse, Anesthesiologist, Physical Therapist or anything in the healthcare field that makes a lot of money. Life has always been about chasing money and be free. I also discovered software engineers were paid really well especially in San Fransisco but cost of living were too much.

I was split between nursing or computer science (or astronaut because I am delusional with my dreams and it's cool).

## Tech Advances

We had an old windows PC when I was in elementary and was only using it to play games like Counter Strike, Dota, Plants vs. Zombies, and so much more. Then I was introduced to the internet around 5th grade, exploring games, watching Youtube, browsing random websites, playing and chatting on Facebook. It is amazing how the tech industry is able to create digital entertainment and connect people around the world!

The Computer AI I experienced back then were purely script based and were easy to beat and predict (or impossible to beat if playing chess). I played with Dota 2 AI, and it was a breeze to win against them. My view to AI completely changed when I witnessed OpenAI beat pro players in Dota 2 on a 1v1. If I only had capital, I would have invested in AI at the time! (Note: but I didn't know NVDIA would be the leader today) OpenAI kept improving and even won against 5v5. It's incredible to see AI beat humans! Hence, my interest in AI began. I explored AI applications and possible future advancements. 

## What to do after highschool?

Of course I was not an amazing student but could at least comprehend mathematics. Knowing that UIUC was an amazing school for CS and close to home, I gave my best shot to be admitted at the school one day. I kept getting beat by college exams, about to exhaust my free attempts on ACTs and SATs. I never had amazing scores. I could not excel in English section. I was only good with math and only took 2 advanced courses: AP Physics and AP Computer Science. I felt that I was behind with my classmates who share the dream of getting to prestigious schools. I thought I was not enough. **Comparison is truly the thief of joy.**

### But why do I even want to pursue Computer Science? 

I already had an internal fallback plan of pursuing nursing if I fail to pursue Computer Science. Besides, I would love to help people in improving their health, and I am passionate in being healthy. I am sure that it will be a struggle to handle memorization, the necessary prerequisites and the stress of being a nurse. However, I know I am good with math and logical reasoning, and I should hone the skills I am good at. Therefore, I continued to pursue my goal of getting in to Computer Science at UIUC where the chances feel low but wanted to conquer it through hardwork. 

But I keep asking myself why Computer Science? There are plenty of career paths I could've taken. In my AP Physics class, I was in the same group project with a classmate who is really smart and shares the same goal as I do. So, I asked him the same question.

"I want to make the world a better place." He said.

In my head, I was singing "Heal the World" by Michael Jackson.

I couldn't help but feel a mixture of awe and uncertainty. Could one person really change the world? Is that even possible with CS? Could I make a difference too?

**I want to make the world a better place too!**
